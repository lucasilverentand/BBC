Icon Composer composition
=========================
Native sky gradient, a photo-backed Clouds group, and a separate Message.png
foreground. The current composition was adjusted and saved in Icon Composer by Luca;
preserve its layer placement and native material settings. The white filled message bubble is Apple's SF Symbols "message.fill" glyph, rendered
by script/render_message_symbol.swift; its shine, refraction and shadow come from
Icon Composer. It has no painted highlights. Groups are stored front to back.

Regenerate the filled white bubble:
xcrun swiftc -j 1 -parse-as-library script/render_message_symbol.swift -o /tmp/bbc-message-symbol
/tmp/bbc-message-symbol Assets/AppIcon.icon/Assets/Message.png

Build icon resources: bash script/prepare_icon.sh

The cloud photograph was imported by Luca in Icon Composer as
AppIcon.icon/Assets/getty-images-dZH5qcz-DhI-unsplash.jpg, replacing the earlier
generated transparent cloud artwork.

Source: AppIcon.icon/icon.json and its referenced files in AppIcon.icon/Assets/.
AppIcon.icns and AppIcon.png are generated fallback/preview assets; the compiled
asset catalog provides high-resolution renditions and native appearance modes.
