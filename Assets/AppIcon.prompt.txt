Icon Composer composition
=========================
Native sky gradient, a transparent Clouds.png group, and a separate Message.png
foreground. The white filled message bubble is Apple's SF Symbols "message.fill" glyph, rendered
by script/render_message_symbol.swift; its shine, refraction and shadow come from
Icon Composer. It has no painted highlights. Groups are stored front to back.

Regenerate the filled white bubble:
xcrun swiftc -j 1 -parse-as-library script/render_message_symbol.swift -o /tmp/bbc-message-symbol
/tmp/bbc-message-symbol Assets/AppIcon.icon/Assets/Message.png

Build icon resources: bash script/prepare_icon.sh

Cloud layer created with the built-in image-generation tool. Final edit prompt:
Extract the soft white fluffy clouds from the previous BBC artwork. Remove the
entire blue message bubble, tail, highlights and shadows. Remove all blue sky and
leave genuine alpha transparency between and above the clouds. Preserve luminous
white cloud texture, with large banks across the bottom half and sides and a
mostly clear centre for the separate message symbol. Complete previously covered
clouds naturally. No speech bubble, symbol, frame, rounded mask, exterior shadow
or opaque background. Only cloud forms with natural soft alpha edges.

Source: AppIcon.icon/icon.json and AppIcon.icon/Assets/{Clouds,Message}.png.
AppIcon.icns and AppIcon.png are generated fallback/preview assets; the compiled
asset catalog provides high-resolution renditions and native appearance modes.
