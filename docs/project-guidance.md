Read the relevant topic alongside its existing implementation contract. Preserve explicit scope and qualifications; historical task authorizations do not grant new operational authority.

This is a public product repository. Keep personal sending accounts, recipient identities, credentials and private deployment details out of its documentation. Operators configure their own account and client access locally.
